Inspect the model

Deterministic scenario arithmetic. Not a trained forecasting model or a production queueing engine.

Uncorrected demand/hour = 80 × (1 + surge%) × repeat-contact factor Corrected demand/hour = 80 + 80 × surge% × (1 − correction effectiveness) EMEA capacity/hour = 50 agents × available fraction × 75% productive time × 85% occupancy × 60 / handling minutes Transferred capacity = eligible agents after donor reserve × the same productivity factors Unserved hours = max(0, demand − available capacity) × incident hours × handling minutes / 60 Recovery cost = unserved hours × $42 overtime rate + selected intervention costs Forecast labor% = ($95,000 baseline labor + recovery cost) / scenario revenue Contribution = revenue − $300,000 other modeled delivery costs − forecast labor Contribution preserved = selected contribution − unmanaged contribution

Recovery cost assumes remaining work is ultimately cleared using overtime. It is a planning estimate, not spend already incurred. Approved correction labor costs $2,000 and reassignment coordination labor costs $500 in this model. The 22% labor ceiling is illustrative, not a Unisys benchmark. No breach probability or service credit is invented; individual deadlines and verified contract terms would be needed.
